Gender annotation

The CoreNLP gender annotator uses a neural network (coref) approach with roughly 60% accuracy; it requires a great deal of memory.

The dictionary csv must have at least two columns labeled 'Name' and 'Gender' (case-sensitive values 'Male'/'Female', or 'F'/'M' for US Social Security files).

US Social Security names

Plots the use of first names by US state/year of birth from the US Social Security databases. Not yet available: the lib/namesGender data files have not been ported from the desktop NLP Suite.
